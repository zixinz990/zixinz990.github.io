<div class="bio-text" markdown="1">

I am Zixin Zhang (张子昕), a fourth-year Ph.D. candidate in Mechanical Engineering at [Center for Robotics and Biosystems](https://robotics.northwestern.edu/), [Northwestern University](https://www.northwestern.edu/), advised by [Dr. Todd Murphey](https://murpheylab.github.io/people/toddmurphey.html).

I was a full-time research intern on the HALO team at the [Robotics and AI Institute](https://rai-inst.com/) for five months, during which time I conducted research on humanoid loco-manipulation and reinforcement learning, working with [Dr. Matt Estrada](https://estrada1.github.io/), [Dr. Jean-Pierre Sleiman](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=FW-U2M8AAAAJ&hl=en), [Dr. Farbod Farshidian](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=ciragesAAAAJ&hl=en), and [Dr. Sylvain Bertrand](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sylvain-bertrand-robotics/).

I got my Master of Science degree in Mechanical Engineering at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cmu.edu/) in 2023, advised by [Dr. Zachary Manchester](https://roboticexplorationlab.org/) at the Robotics Institute. I graduated from [Southern University of Science and Technology](https://www.sustech.edu.cn/en/) with a Bachelor of Engineering degree in Robotics Engineering in 2021, and my advisor was [Dr. Chaoyang Song](https://mbzuai.ac.ae/study/faculty/chaoyang-song/).

Outside of school, I am a lover of [piano](/piano/), travel, skiing, and [photography](/photography/). I've been to a lot of places (check out my [footprint map](#my-footprints) below), but I haven't been to Europe, Oceania, South America, or Antarctica. Someday I will be there!
